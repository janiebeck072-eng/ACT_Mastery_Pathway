# ACT Mastery Pathway — Product Overview

## Product promise
Diagnose what a student already knows, compact mastered content, teach only the remaining skills, require independent mastery, and measure transfer on a parallel post-assessment.

## Student journey
1. 45-item / 50-minute diagnostic.
2. Automatic subtopic profile across 15 skills.
3. Diagnostic compaction for subtopics demonstrated at the mastery threshold.
4. Personalized study plan prioritizing weakest unmastered skills.
5. Each module: worked example → ACT lens → strategy-first guided practice → 5-item mastery gate.
6. Brief retrieval/brain-break activities after milestone completions.
7. Locked post-assessment after all modules are mastered.
8. Printable mastery report and completion certificate.

## What the prototype measures
- Diagnostic accuracy by subtopic
- Module completion and mastery attempts
- Guided strategy-selection accuracy
- Diagnostic-compacted vs instruction-mastered modules
- Post-assessment performance and raw-score growth

## Commercial positioning
This is an independent ACT-style instructional curriculum, not official ACT material and not affiliated with ACT. A production license should add secure accounts, organization-level rosters, server-side data, license enforcement, and a substantially expanded QA-reviewed item bank.
