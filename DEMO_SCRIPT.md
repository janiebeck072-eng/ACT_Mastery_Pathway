# 7-Minute Boss / Buyer Demo Script

1. **Problem (45 sec):** Most ACT programs give every student the same sequence, even when students have different gaps.
2. **Diagnostic (60 sec):** Show the timed 45-item assessment, navigator, flagging, and autosave. Explain that no tutoring feedback appears during assessment.
3. **Adaptive pathway (60 sec):** Submit/demo a diagnostic and show mastered skills compacted while remaining modules are prioritized.
4. **Instructional differentiator (2 min):** Open a module. Show worked example, ACT lens, strategy-first question, corrective strategy feedback that does not reveal the answer, then solving.
5. **Mastery gate (60 sec):** Show 5-item unscaffolded check and 80% gate with fresh retake values.
6. **Data story (60 sec):** Show instructor view, strategy accuracy, mastery attempts, diagnostic-compacted vs learned skills, and printable report.
7. **Outcome (15 sec):** Show locked post-assessment and certificate. Close with: Diagnose → personalize → teach strategy → prove mastery → measure growth.
