# Instructor Quick Start

## Before students begin
- Give students a quiet 50-minute diagnostic window.
- Encourage authentic effort; the diagnostic controls what instruction they can skip.
- Do not coach individual items during the diagnostic.

## After the diagnostic
Open the module dashboard. Green modules were compacted from demonstrated mastery. Students should begin with the system's recommended lowest-performing unmastered subtopic or follow the tutor's assigned order.

## During a module
The tutor's role is to listen for strategy language. Ask students to name the structure they see before calculating. The software intentionally withholds the answer during strategy feedback.

## Mastery checks
Students need 4/5. A failed check is diagnostic information, not a penalty. Have the student revisit the worked example/common trap, then take a fresh generated version.

## Intervention protocol
When a student repeatedly misses a module:
1. Identify whether the error is strategy selection or execution.
2. Re-model one example aloud.
3. Have the student explain the next example in words before computing.
4. Assign a fresh guided set.
5. Reattempt mastery only when the student can name the method independently.

## Completion conference
After the post-assessment, print the mastery report. Discuss diagnostic-to-post growth, remaining fragile skills, and a short test-day maintenance plan.
