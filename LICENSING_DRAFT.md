# Licensing Concept — Draft, not legal advice

## Suggested pilot offer
**ACT Mastery Pathway Center License — $1,000/year**

Possible included scope for an initial market test:
- One tutoring-center location
- Up to 100 active student profiles per license year
- Unlimited staff users at that licensed location
- Full personalized ACT Math curriculum
- Diagnostic and post-assessment
- Student mastery reports
- Product updates during the license term
- Email onboarding/support

## Guardrails to define in a real agreement
- License is non-transferable and limited to one physical/business location.
- No redistribution, resale, public reposting, scraping, or repackaging of curriculum content.
- Define active student, renewal date, support scope, data retention, privacy obligations, termination, refunds, and availability.
- Do not promise official ACT score increases. Use evidence from your own pilots.
- Have an attorney review terms, privacy policy, student-data practices, and intellectual-property language before commercial launch.

## Expansion pricing to test later
Do not hard-code this until pilots establish value. Potential dimensions: active students, number of locations, white-label reporting, onboarding/training, and analytics tier.
