# Pilot / QA Checklist

## Before a live pilot
- [ ] Complete every diagnostic item on Chromebook and desktop.
- [ ] Verify exactly one correct answer for every item.
- [ ] Verify every table, SVG, graph, and triangle is legible at 100% and 125% zoom.
- [ ] Complete all 15 modules and intentionally choose each wrong strategy once.
- [ ] Confirm strategy feedback never reveals the numerical answer.
- [ ] Verify every mastery retake generates fresh values.
- [ ] Confirm 4/5 unlocks mastery and 3/5 does not.
- [ ] Confirm post-assessment stays locked until all 15 subtopics are mastered.
- [ ] Print mastery report and certificate to PDF.
- [ ] Test export/import progress between two browsers.
- [ ] Test keyboard navigation and visible focus.
- [ ] Have a second mathematics educator independently review the entire item bank.

## Content QA record recommended for every commercial item
Store: item ID, subtopic, skill, difficulty, correct answer, distractor rationale, strategy tag, solution, reviewer, review date, visual check, accessibility check, source/originality note.
