# ACT Mastery Pathway — Curriculum & Product Guide

## Product promise
ACT Mastery Pathway is an adaptive, mastery-based ACT Math curriculum designed to reduce wasted practice. Students begin with a full diagnostic. Demonstrated skills are compacted; unfinished skills become a personalized sequence of short modules. Each module explicitly teaches strategy recognition before computation, then requires independent mastery.

## Student journey
1. **45-question diagnostic (50 minutes).** Three items are tagged to each of 15 subtopics.
2. **Skill compaction.** A subtopic is pre-mastered only at the configured 80% threshold. With three diagnostic items, the current implementation requires 3/3.
3. **Targeted module.** Worked example → strategy lesson → common trap/test-day habit → three strategy-first guided questions.
4. **Mastery gate.** Five unscaffolded items; 4/5 required. Retakes generate new values.
5. **Post-assessment.** A parallel 45-question ACT-style form unlocks after all modules are complete.
6. **Growth report.** Diagnostic, mastery status, post score, and subtopic evidence are printable.

## Instructional model
Every guided item separates **strategy recognition** from **answer execution**. If the student chooses an incorrect strategy, the system identifies and explains the appropriate approach without revealing the numerical answer. This creates two useful instructional signals: “I did not know what to do” versus “I knew what to do but executed incorrectly.” A production analytics system should store these separately.

## Mastery policy
- Diagnostic pre-mastery: 80% threshold; current 3-item design effectively requires 3/3.
- Module mastery: 4/5.
- Post-assessment: unlocked only after all assigned modules are mastered.
- Scores are instructional indicators and should not be presented as official ACT scale scores without a validated concordance.

## Recommended tutoring-center implementation
A 60–90 minute onboarding/diagnostic session, followed by 2–4 modules per week depending on student need. Tutors use module data to group students for short intervention, while students complete independent guided practice and mastery checks. Reassess with the post form after pathway completion.

## Content quality standard for commercial release
Before paid deployment, every item should receive a documented QA pass for: mathematical correctness, exactly one defensible answer, distractor plausibility, visual fidelity, accessibility, reading load, calculator assumptions, skill tag, difficulty tag, and originality. Geometry/trig/table/graph items require a visual review at desktop and Chromebook widths.
